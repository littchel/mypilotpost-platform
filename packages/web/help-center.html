Frequently asked
What do I need to get started?
You need an email address and a plan subscription to get started. A free trial is available — no credit card required during the trial period. Once you're in, the onboarding flow will guide you through brand setup and your first social platform connection.
Can I manage multiple brands from one account?
Yes. myPilotPost supports multiple brand workspaces under a single account. Each brand is fully isolated — content, analytics, connected accounts, and team members are separate per brand. The number of brands available depends on your plan.
Do I need to be 18 to use myPilotPost?
Yes. myPilotPost is an 18+ platform. Users must be at least 18 years old and legally capable of entering binding contracts. This is required under our Terms and Conditions and applicable South African law.

Frequently asked
Why does myPilotPost need access to my Facebook Page?
Publishing to a Facebook Page requires Page admin access via the Meta Graph API. myPilotPost requests the minimum set of permissions needed: to see your Pages, to publish posts, and to retrieve engagement metrics. We do not access your personal profile, inbox, or ad account. Full scope details: OAuth & Permissions.
My connection shows as expired — what do I do?
Navigate to Control Tower → Integration Hub, locate the expired connection, and click Reconnect. You'll be taken through the OAuth flow again. Most connections expire when: the platform revokes the token (e.g. you changed your password), the token's validity period lapses without a refresh, or you revoked access from within the connected platform's settings.
Can I connect the same platform account to multiple brands?
Each brand workspace has its own isolated set of connected platform accounts. You can connect the same social account to multiple brand workspaces, but the connections and tokens are stored separately per brand. This ensures brand data isolation across all workspaces.

Frequently asked
Why did my scheduled post fail?
Failed deliveries are always logged and surfaced in your Launch Pad delivery history — they are never silently dropped. The most common causes are an expired or revoked OAuth token on the connected platform, an API-level rejection from the target platform (e.g. content policy violation), or a platform-wide outage. Check the Status Page first, then contact support@mypilotpost.com with the post details if the issue persists.
Can myPilotPost AI publish content automatically without my approval?
No. myPilotPost AI never publishes content autonomously. All publishing requires explicit user scheduling and confirmation via the Launch Pad. AI is advisory — it helps draft and recommend content, but cannot trigger publishing on your behalf. See our AI Principles for full details.
What is the 15-minute conflict window?
The scheduling engine enforces a 15-minute protection window between posts targeting the same platform and account. This prevents accidental double-posting or conflicting schedules. If you attempt to schedule two posts to the same account within 15 minutes, the conflict will be flagged before the schedule is confirmed.

Frequently asked
Why is my engagement data not showing?
Analytics data is retrieved from connected platforms via their APIs. Data will only appear if: (1) the relevant platform is connected, (2) the OAuth token is valid, and (3) the platform's API makes that data available. Some metrics require a Business or Creator account on the connected platform. If your token is expired, reconnect from the Integration Hub and the data will sync on the next retrieval cycle.
Are the analytics numbers estimated or actual?
All metrics displayed in Mission Analytics are real data retrieved directly from connected platform APIs. myPilotPost does not generate estimated or modelled engagement figures. If a platform's API does not provide a specific metric, it is not displayed rather than substituted with an estimate.

Frequently asked
Can I get a refund?
Yes, within the periods defined in our Refund Policy. Monthly subscriptions: full refund within 3 days; partial refund from day 4–14. Annual subscriptions: full refund within 14 days of initial payment; 50% within 14 days of renewal. Renewals outside these windows are non-refundable. Email support@mypilotpost.com with "Refund Request" in the subject line.
Does myPilotPost store my payment card details?
No. Payments are processed entirely by YOCO, a South African licensed payment service provider. myPilotPost never stores, processes, or has access to your card number, CVV, or banking credentials. YOCO handles all card data within their PCI-DSS compliant environment.
How will I know my annual plan is about to renew?
Annual subscription renewal reminders are sent 14 days and 7 days before your renewal date to the email address on your account. This gives you time to review your plan, make changes, or cancel before the renewal processes.

Frequently asked
How do I request a copy of my personal data?
Email privacy@mypilotpost.com with your account email address and the request type (access, correction, deletion, or portability). We acknowledge within 5 business days and respond fully within 30 days, as required by POPIA and GDPR.
What happens to my data when I delete my account?
Personal data is removed within 30 days of a confirmed deletion request. OAuth tokens are deleted immediately. Payment transaction references are retained for 5 years for accounting obligations under South African law. Anonymised, aggregated data (from which you cannot be identified) may be retained beyond that period. Full details in Data Handling.
Can I transfer a brand to another user?
Brand ownership transfers are not currently self-service. Contact support@mypilotpost.com if you need to transfer ownership of a brand workspace to a different account.
