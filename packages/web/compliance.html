Compliance

GDPR, POPIA, PAIA, ECTA, CPA, cookie consent, and data rights — plain-language compliance documentation for myPilotPost.

Thuthu (Pty) Ltd t/a myPilotPost  ·  Reg. No. 2025/102758/07  ·  Information Officer: Littchel Nhlalo Mathuthu  ·  legal@mypilotpost.com

myPilotPost serves users globally. Our legal foundation is South African law. We also comply with GDPR for EU residents and applicable privacy standards for all other jurisdictions. This page provides a plain-language compliance overview. Our full legal documents — Privacy Policy, Terms and Conditions, GDPR Compliance Statement, and Refund Policy — remain the authoritative legal instruments. In any conflict between this page and those documents, the relevant legal document governs.

South African Legal Framework

POPIA — Protection of Personal Information Act, No. 4 of 2013

POPIA is the primary data protection legislation governing how Thuthu (Pty) Ltd collects, processes, stores, and shares personal information. We are required to comply with POPIA's eight lawful processing conditions:

POPIA Condition How myPilotPost Complies
Accountability Thuthu (Pty) Ltd takes full responsibility for all personal information processed through myPilotPost. The Information Officer is Littchel Nhlalo Mathuthu.
Processing limitation We collect only what is necessary to operate the platform. See Data Handling for the full inventory.
Purpose specification Data is collected for specified, explicit, and lawful purposes as documented in our Privacy Policy. It is not processed beyond those purposes.
Further processing limitation We do not repurpose personal data for uses incompatible with the original stated purpose.
Information quality We maintain accurate records and enable users to request corrections at any time.
Openness This Trust Center, our Privacy Policy, and our PAIA Manual document all processing activities transparently.
Security safeguards Encryption in transit and at rest, brand-scoped isolation, JWT authentication, rate limiting, and monitoring are all in place. See Security.
Data subject participation Users can access, correct, export, and request deletion of their data. See Your Rights below.

PAIA — Promotion of Access to Information Act, No. 2 of 2000

PAIA gives any person the right to request access to records held by a private body where that access is required to exercise or protect a right. Thuthu (Pty) Ltd has compiled a PAIA Manual as required by section 51 of PAIA.

  • PAIA Manual available at mypilotpost.com/images/PAIA_Manual.pdf
  • To submit a PAIA request: Email legal@mypilotpost.com with subject line "PAIA Request", attaching completed Form 2 (available from the Information Regulator at inforeg.org.za)
  • Response timeframe: 30 days from receipt of a valid request, extendable by 30 days in complex cases as permitted by section 26 of PAIA
  • If a request is refused, we will notify the requester in writing with reasons as required by PAIA

Information Regulator of South Africa: inforeg.org.za  ·  inforeg@justice.gov.za

CPA — Consumer Protection Act, No. 68 of 2008

  • Right to disclosure: Pricing, subscription terms, and refund conditions are disclosed clearly before purchase
  • Right to fair contract terms: Our Terms and Conditions do not contain provisions that are unfair, unreasonable, or unjust within the meaning of the CPA
  • Right to cancel: Users may cancel their subscription at any time in accordance with our Refund Policy
  • Cooling-off rights: Our Refund Policy provides a cooling-off period consistent with CPA requirements
  • Dispute resolution: Users may refer disputes to the Consumer Protection Commission of South Africa

ECTA — Electronic Communications and Transactions Act, No. 25 of 2002

  • Displaying company registration and contact details on the website and in legal documents
  • Providing users with a copy of the agreement upon subscription
  • Implementing secure electronic transaction mechanisms via YOCO
  • Maintaining records of electronic transactions in accordance with section 11 of ECTA

GDPR — General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679

GDPR applies to processing of personal data of individuals in the EU and EEA, regardless of where the processing organisation is located. As myPilotPost serves global users, we comply with GDPR for EU residents. For full details see our GDPR Compliance Statement.

Our Role Under GDPR

Thuthu (Pty) Ltd acts as a Data Controller for personal data collected directly from users (account data, usage data). Where users connect third-party platforms and we process content on their behalf, we may also act as a Data Processor in relation to that content data.

Lawful Basis for Processing

Processing Activity Lawful Basis (GDPR Article 6)
Account creation and management Article 6(1)(b) — Contract
Subscription billing via YOCO Article 6(1)(b) — Contract
Content scheduling and delivery Article 6(1)(b) — Contract
Security monitoring and logging Article 6(1)(f) — Legitimate interest
Analytics processing Article 6(1)(f) — Legitimate interest
Marketing communications Article 6(1)(a) — Consent
Legal compliance Article 6(1)(c) — Legal obligation

International Data Transfers

As a South African company using Cloudflare's global infrastructure, personal data of EU residents may be transferred to countries outside the EEA. We implement Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission to ensure adequate protection for such transfers in accordance with GDPR Chapter V.

Data Processing Agreement (DPA)

A GDPR-compliant Data Processing Agreement is available upon request. To request a DPA, email legal@mypilotpost.com — we will countersign and return an executed copy within 10 business days.

Cookie & Analytics Consent

Cookie Categories

Category Basis Description
Strictly Necessary No consent required Required for the platform to function — authentication, session management, security
Analytics (Google Analytics 4) Consent required Usage analytics to understand how the platform is used; IP anonymisation enabled
Functional Consent required Preference storage, UI personalisation

How Consent Works

  • A consent banner is displayed on first visit to mypilotpost.com
  • Google Analytics 4 is not initialised until analytics consent is explicitly granted
  • Consent preferences are stored and respected across sessions
  • Users may withdraw or change consent at any time via the cookie preference panel
  • Consent records including timestamp and version are retained in accordance with legal requirements
  • IP anonymisation is enabled for all GA4 tracking

Email Compliance

  • All marketing emails contain a clear, functioning unsubscribe link
  • Transactional emails (delivery notifications, password reset, billing receipts) are not used for promotional purposes
  • Sender identity (Thuthu (Pty) Ltd t/a myPilotPost) and physical address are included in all commercial communications
  • Unsubscribe requests are actioned within 5 business days
  • Email lists are never sold, rented, or shared with third parties for marketing purposes
  • Marketing emails are sent only to users who have provided explicit consent

Refunds & Billing Compliance

myPilotPost offers subscription-based pricing. Our billing and refund practices comply with the CPA and are documented in full in our Refund Policy.

  • Monthly subscriptions: full refund within 3 days of initial payment; partial pro-rated refund from day 4 to day 14; no refunds on renewals
  • Annual subscriptions: full refund within 14 days of initial payment; 50% refund within 14 days of renewal
  • Billing reminders for annual renewals are sent 14 days and 7 days in advance
  • Payments are processed by YOCO; Thuthu (Pty) Ltd holds a signed merchant agreement with YOCO
  • Consumer statutory rights under the CPA are not affected by this policy

How to Submit a Data Request

Email privacy@mypilotpost.com with your account email address and the nature of your request. We acknowledge within 5 business days and respond fully within 30 days.
Request Type What We Will Do
Access request Provide a full export of personal data we hold about you
Deletion request Delete your account and all associated personal data within 30 days
Correction request Correct inaccurate information on your account
Portability request Export your content, analytics data, and brand configuration in a machine-readable format
Consent withdrawal Process immediately; does not affect prior lawful processing
Objection Assess and respond within 30 days; we will cease processing unless we have compelling legitimate grounds
PAIA record request Process in accordance with PAIA; direct to legal@mypilotpost.com

If you are not satisfied with our response:
South African users: Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator — inforeg.org.za  ·  inforeg@justice.gov.za
EU users: Lodge a complaint with your national data protection supervisory authority
Consumer disputes: Contact the Consumer Protection Commission of South Africa — thencc.org.za
