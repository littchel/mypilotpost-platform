Frequently asked questions

How do I connect a social media platform?
Navigate to Control Tower → Integration Hub and select the platform you wish to connect. You will be redirected to that platform's official OAuth screen — your password is never shared with myPilotPost. See our OAuth & Permissions page for the exact scopes requested per platform.
Can I cancel or change my subscription?
Yes — you can cancel your subscription at any time from your account settings. See our Refund Policy for the full terms, including cooling-off periods and pro-rated refund conditions. Payments are processed by YOCO.
What happens to my data if I delete my account?
Your personal data is removed within 30 days of a confirmed deletion request as required by POPIA and GDPR. OAuth tokens are deleted immediately upon disconnection or account deletion. Payment transaction references are retained for 5 years for tax and accounting obligations. See our Data Handling page for the full retention schedule.
Why did my scheduled post fail to publish?
Delivery failures surface immediately in your Mission Analytics dashboard and Launch Pad delivery history — they are never silently dropped. The most common causes are: an expired or revoked OAuth token on the connected platform, an API-side error from the target platform, or a network interruption at the Cloudflare edge. Check the Status Page to see if there is an active platform incident, then email support@mypilotpost.com with the post ID and delivery log details if the issue persists.
How do I request access to my personal data?
Email privacy@mypilotpost.com with your account email address and the nature of your request (access, correction, deletion, or portability). We will acknowledge within 5 business days and respond fully within 30 days as required by POPIA and GDPR.
Where are myPilotPost's legal documents?
All legal documents are available from the footer: Privacy Policy, Terms and Conditions, Refund Policy, GDPR Compliance Statement, and the PAIA Manual. The full Trust Center covers security, AI principles, data handling, infrastructure, compliance, and OAuth permissions.
