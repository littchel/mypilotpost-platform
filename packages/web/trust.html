Core Trust Principles

  • Brand-scoped isolation — your data is never shared across brands or customers
  • Deterministic scheduling — your content publishes as configured, every time
  • Explainable intelligence — AI assists, explains, and surfaces recommendations; it never acts alone
  • Operational transparency — admin systems are separated from customer workflows and cannot silently alter your state
  • Human-reviewed workflows — no automated publishing without user confirmation
  • Fail-safe delivery architecture — errors surface to you; they do not silently disappear
  • Advisory AI systems — machine learning advises, never decides
  • 18+ platform — users must be 18 or older and legally capable of entering binding contracts
