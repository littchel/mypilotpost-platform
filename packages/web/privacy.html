Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how myPilotPost (Pty) Ltd collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal information. We are committed to handling your data with transparency and in compliance with applicable data protection laws, including GDPR and POPIA.

1. Data Controller

myPilotPost (Pty) Ltd is the data controller for personal information processed through this platform. Our POPIA Information Officer can be reached at privacy@mypilotpost.com.

2. Data We Collect

Account Information

  • Name and email address
  • Password (stored as a cryptographic hash — never in plaintext)
  • Account creation date and last login timestamp
  • Subscription status and plan tier

Brand & Content Data

  • Brand name, description, voice settings, and goals you configure
  • Social media posts, drafts, and scheduling records
  • Campaign briefs and content history (within your brand only)

Usage & Technical Data

  • IP addresses and device information (for security and rate limiting)
  • API request logs (retained 90 days for debugging and security)
  • Delivery records and scheduling logs

OAuth Integration Data

  • Connected account identifiers and platform usernames
  • OAuth access tokens (encrypted, scoped per brand)
  • Permission scopes granted at connection time

Analytics Data

  • Engagement metrics from connected platform APIs (post reach, likes, etc.)
  • Delivery success and failure events

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and operate the platform (scheduling, delivery, analytics)
  • To authenticate your account and maintain session security
  • To provide AI-assisted content features within your brand context
  • To send transactional communications (delivery alerts, billing notifications)
  • To send marketing communications where you have given consent
  • To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents
  • To comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use your data to train AI models for other customers or for third-party advertising.

4. Lawful Basis for Processing

  • Contract: Account management, scheduling, delivery, and core platform features
  • Legitimate interest: Security monitoring, error logging, analytics for service improvement
  • Consent: Marketing emails, analytics cookies
  • Legal obligation: Retention of billing records, responding to lawful requests

5. OAuth & Third-Party Platforms

When you connect a social media or productivity platform, we receive OAuth tokens — not your password. These tokens are:

  • Encrypted before storage
  • Scoped to the permissions you approved
  • Never shared with third parties
  • Immediately deleted when you disconnect the integration

See our full OAuth & Permissions page for per-platform details.

6. Retention Policy

  • Account data: retained while your account is active
  • Delivery logs: 90 days
  • API request logs: 90 days
  • Analytics data: duration of subscription
  • Upon account deletion: personal data removed within 30 days; billing records retained in anonymised form as required by law

7. Subprocessors

We use the following third-party processors to deliver the platform:

  • Cloudflare Inc. — hosting, edge compute, database, media storage
  • Resend — transactional email delivery
  • Yoco — payment processing (we do not store card details)
  • Groq / AI providers — AI content generation (prompts are not shared across customers)
  • Google Analytics (GA4) — usage analytics (consent required; IP anonymization enabled)

8. Cookies & Analytics

  • Strictly necessary cookies: Required for authentication and platform function
  • Analytics cookies (GA4): Only activated after explicit consent
  • IP anonymization: Enabled for all analytics tracking

You can manage your cookie preferences via the consent banner or Settings → Privacy & Data in your dashboard.

9. International Transfers

myPilotPost operates on Cloudflare's global edge network. Your data may be processed in data centers outside South Africa, including in the United States and European Union. We rely on Cloudflare's standard contractual clauses and adequacy mechanisms for international transfers in compliance with GDPR and POPIA.

10. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access — request a copy of your data (available via Settings → Privacy & Data)
  • Rectification — correct inaccurate data
  • Erasure — request deletion of your account (available via Settings → Privacy & Data)
  • Portability — export your data in JSON format
  • Restriction — limit processing in specific circumstances
  • Objection — object to processing based on legitimate interest
  • Withdraw consent — for consent-based processing (marketing, analytics)

To exercise any right, email privacy@mypilotpost.com. We respond within 30 days.

If you are dissatisfied with our response, you may lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator (South Africa): inforeg.org.za

11. Security Measures

We protect your data using:

  • TLS/HTTPS encryption for all data in transit
  • Encryption at rest via Cloudflare's storage layer
  • Brand-scoped data isolation enforced at the API layer
  • JWT-based session management with server-side validation
  • Rate limiting, DDoS protection, and security headers on all endpoints

See our Security page for full details.

12. Children's Privacy

myPilotPost is not directed at children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe we have inadvertently collected data from a minor, contact privacy@mypilotpost.com and we will delete it promptly.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated via email or a notice in the platform. The updated policy will always be available at this URL. Continued use of the platform after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

14. Contact

For privacy enquiries, data requests, or to exercise your rights:

  • Email: privacy@mypilotpost.com
  • Subject line: Privacy Request — [type of request]

We acknowledge all requests within 5 business days.
