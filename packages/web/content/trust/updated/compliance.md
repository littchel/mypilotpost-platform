### PAIA — Promotion of Access to Information Act, No. 2 of 2000

PAIA gives any person the right to request access to records held by a private body where that access is required to exercise or protect a right. Thuthu (Pty) Ltd has compiled a PAIA Manual as required by section 51 of PAIA.

- **PAIA Manual:** Available at [mypilotpost.com/images/PAIA_Manual.pdf](https://www.mypilotpost.com/images/PAIA_Manual.pdf)
- **To submit a PAIA request:** Email legal@mypilotpost.com with the subject line *PAIA Request*, attaching completed Form 2 (available from the Information Regulator at [inforeg.org.za](https://www.inforeg.org.za))
- **Response timeframe:** 30 days from receipt of a valid request, extendable by 30 days in complex cases as permitted by section 26 of PAIA
- **Refusal:** If a request is refused, we will notify the requester in writing with reasons as required by PAIA

**Information Regulator of South Africa:** [inforeg.org.za](https://www.inforeg.org.za) · inforeg@justice.gov.za

### CPA — Consumer Protection Act, No. 68 of 2008

myPilotPost is a service offered to consumers and businesses in South Africa. Our practices comply with the CPA, including:

- **Right to disclosure:** Pricing, subscription terms, and refund conditions are disclosed clearly before purchase
- **Right to fair contract terms:** Our Terms and Conditions do not contain provisions that are unfair, unreasonable, or unjust within the meaning of the CPA
- **Right to cancel:** Users may cancel their subscription at any time in accordance with our [Refund Policy](/refund-policy)
- **Cooling-off rights:** Our Refund Policy provides a cooling-off period consistent with CPA requirements for direct marketing transactions
- **Dispute resolution:** Users may refer disputes to the Consumer Protection Commission of South Africa if not resolved through our internal process

### ECTA — Electronic Communications and Transactions Act, No. 25 of 2002

As an electronic commerce platform, myPilotPost complies with ECTA, including:

- Displaying company registration and contact details on the website and in legal documents
- Providing users with a copy of the agreement upon subscription
- Implementing secure electronic transaction mechanisms via YOCO
- Maintaining records of electronic transactions in accordance with section 11 of ECTA

## GDPR — General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679

GDPR applies to processing of personal data of individuals in the EU and EEA, regardless of where the processing organisation is located. As myPilotPost serves global users, we comply with GDPR for EU residents.

For full details see our [GDPR Compliance Statement](/gdpr-compliance).

### Our Role Under GDPR

Thuthu (Pty) Ltd acts as a **Data Controller** for personal data collected directly from users (account data, usage data). Where users connect third-party platforms and we process content on their behalf, we may also act as a **Data Processor** in relation to that content data.

| Subscription billing via YOCO | Article 6(1)(b) — Contract |

As a South African company using Cloudflare's global infrastructure, personal data of EU residents may be transferred to countries outside the EEA. We implement Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission to ensure adequate protection for such transfers in accordance with GDPR Chapter V.

### Data Processing Agreement (DPA)

A GDPR-compliant Data Processing Agreement is available upon request. To request a DPA, email legal@mypilotpost.com — we will countersign and return an executed copy within 10 business days.

## Email Compliance

All marketing and promotional emails sent by myPilotPost comply with POPIA, ECTA, and where applicable, the CAN-SPAM Act and GDPR:

- All marketing emails contain a clear, functioning unsubscribe link
- Transactional emails (delivery notifications, password reset, billing receipts) are exempt from marketing opt-out but are not used for promotional purposes
- Sender identity (Thuthu (Pty) Ltd t/a myPilotPost) and physical address are included in all commercial communications
- Unsubscribe requests are actioned within 5 business days
- Email lists are never sold, rented, or shared with third parties for marketing purposes
- Marketing emails are sent only to users who have provided explicit consent

## Refunds & Billing Compliance

myPilotPost offers subscription-based pricing. Our billing and refund practices comply with the CPA and are documented in full in our [Refund Policy](/refund-policy). Key terms:

- Monthly subscriptions: full refund within 3 days of initial payment; partial pro-rated refund from day 4 to day 14; no refunds on renewals
- Annual subscriptions: full refund within 14 days of initial payment; 50% refund within 14 days of renewal
- Billing reminders for annual renewals are sent 14 days and 7 days in advance
- Payments are processed by YOCO; Thuthu (Pty) Ltd holds a signed merchant agreement with YOCO
- Consumer statutory rights under the CPA are not affected by this policy

## How to Submit a Data Request

> Email **privacy@mypilotpost.com** with your account email address and the nature of your request. We acknowledge within 5 business days and respond fully within 30 days.

| Request Type | What We Will Do |
|---|---|
| Access request | Provide a full export of personal data we hold about you |
| Deletion request | Delete your account and all associated personal data within 30 days |
| Correction request | Correct inaccurate information on your account |
| Portability request | Export your content, analytics data, and brand configuration in a machine-readable format |
| Consent withdrawal | Process immediately; does not affect prior lawful processing |
| Objection | Assess and respond within 30 days; we will cease processing unless we have compelling legitimate grounds |
| PAIA record request | Process in accordance with PAIA; direct to legal@mypilotpost.com |

**If you are not satisfied with our response:**

- **South African users:** Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator — [inforeg.org.za](https://www.inforeg.org.za) · inforeg@justice.gov.za
- **EU users:** Lodge a complaint with your national data protection supervisory authority
- **Consumer disputes:** Contact the Consumer Protection Commission of South Africa — [thencc.org.za](https://www.thencc.org.za)
