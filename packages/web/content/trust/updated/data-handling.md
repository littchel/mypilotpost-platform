This page explains what data myPilotPost collects, how it is used, what it is never used for, and how it is protected. Thuthu (Pty) Ltd is committed to data minimisation — we collect only what is necessary to deliver and operate the platform — as required by section 10 of POPIA (purpose specification) and GDPR Article 5(1)(c) (data minimisation).

---

## What Data We Collect

### Account Data

- Name and surname
- Email address
- Phone number (where provided)
- Date of birth (to verify the 18+ eligibility requirement)
- Company name and details, job title and role (where provided)
- Profile picture (where provided)
- Authentication identifiers from OAuth providers (where applicable)
- Password hash where email/password authentication is used — passwords are never stored in plaintext

### Brand & Workspace Data

- Brand name, description, and voice configuration
- Platform targeting preferences and strategic content goals
- Brand memory events — patterns and preferences you configure within your workspace
- Team member associations (which users are authorised to access which brands)

### Content Data

- Social media post drafts and scheduling records — text, media references, platform target, and status
- Blog post drafts and publication records
- Campaign configurations and mission briefs
- Content generation history (prompts and AI-generated outputs) — used only to improve AI assistance within your own brand workspace

### Scheduling & Delivery Data

- Scheduled post records — target platform, scheduled UTC time, current status
- Delivery results — success, failure, and retry records
- Delivery job logs — used for reliability monitoring and error resolution

### Analytics Data

- Engagement metrics retrieved from connected platform APIs — including but not limited to likes, shares, reach, impressions, and follower data, as made available by each connected platform
- Delivery success and failure events
- Content performance comparisons (within your brand workspace only)

### OAuth & Integration Metadata

- Connected account identifiers — we never receive social platform passwords
- OAuth access tokens — encrypted at rest, scoped per connected account and brand
- Integration status and health indicators
- Permission scopes granted at the time of OAuth connection

### Payment Data

Payments are processed by YOCO. We receive and store:

- Subscription tier and plan details
- Payment status (successful, failed, pending)
- Transaction reference identifiers from YOCO

We do not store, process, or have access to payment card numbers, CVV codes, or banking credentials. All card data is handled exclusively by YOCO within their PCI-DSS compliant environment.

### Technical & Operational Data

- API request logs — used for debugging, rate limiting, and security monitoring
- Error logs — used for reliability and incident response
- IP address and device information — collected automatically when you use the platform
- Browser type and version, operating system
- Pages visited, time spent, and navigation metadata
- Cookie and tracking technology data — subject to your consent choices (see [Compliance](/compliance))

---

## What We Do NOT Collect

> We do not collect data we do not need. This is a legal obligation under POPIA section 10 and GDPR Article 5(1)(c), and a product commitment.

- Social media account passwords — OAuth means we never receive them
- Private messages, DMs, or inbox content from any connected social platform
- Browsing activity outside of myPilotPost
- Device fingerprints or persistent cross-site tracking identifiers
- Third-party advertising profiles or ad network data
- Data from platforms you have not explicitly connected to your myPilotPost account
- Content data from other customers or brands
- Pilot licence data, logbook summaries, or aviation credentials — myPilotPost is a social media management platform, not an aviation platform
- Biometric data or government-issued identity document numbers (except where legally required for a specific compliance purpose)

## Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

We share data only in the following limited circumstances:

- **YOCO** — payment processing; governed by YOCO's own terms and privacy policy
- **Cloudflare** — infrastructure provider hosting our API, database, storage, and frontend; data is processed under Cloudflare's data processing terms
- **Connected social platforms** — when you schedule and publish content, the relevant content and OAuth token are transmitted to that platform's API
- **Legal requirement** — where compelled by a valid court order, subpoena, or applicable South African law or equivalent international legal process
- **Business transfers** — in the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets; users will be notified in advance

|---|---|

Anonymised, aggregated analytics data (from which individuals cannot be identified) may be retained beyond these periods for platform improvement purposes.

## Your Data Rights

Under POPIA and GDPR you have the right to:

- **Access** — request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
- **Rectification** — request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
- **Deletion / Erasure** — request deletion of your account and associated personal data
- **Portability** — request an export of your content, analytics data, and brand configuration in a machine-readable format
- **Restriction** — request that we limit processing of your data in specific circumstances
- **Objection** — object to processing based on legitimate interests
- **Withdraw consent** — withdraw marketing or analytics consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing

**To exercise any right:** Email privacy@mypilotpost.com with your account email address and the nature of your request. We will acknowledge within 5 business days and respond fully within 30 days as required by POPIA and GDPR.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may lodge a complaint with:

- **Information Regulator (South Africa):** inforeg.org.za · inforeg@justice.gov.za
- **EU residents:** Your local national data protection supervisory authority
