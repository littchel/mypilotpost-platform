# Compliance

**Route:** `/compliance`

myPilotPost is designed to meet the regulatory requirements applicable to a global SaaS platform serving customers in South Africa and internationally. This page documents our posture under GDPR, POPIA, and PAIA.

---

## GDPR — General Data Protection Regulation

GDPR applies to any organisation processing personal data of EU residents, regardless of where the organisation is based.

### Lawful Basis for Processing

| Processing Activity | Lawful Basis |
|---|---|
| Account creation & management | Contract — necessary to provide the service |
| Scheduling & delivery | Contract — core service delivery |
| Analytics processing | Legitimate interest — service improvement and reliability |
| Marketing emails | Consent — opt-in required, easily withdrawn |
| Error & security logging | Legitimate interest — security and operational integrity |

### Your GDPR Rights

- **Right of access** — receive a copy of your personal data
- **Right to rectification** — correct inaccurate data
- **Right to erasure** — request deletion of your data
- **Right to data portability** — export your data in a machine-readable format
- **Right to restrict processing** — limit how we use your data
- **Right to object** — object to processing based on legitimate interest
- **Rights related to automated decision-making** — we do not make solely automated decisions with legal effects

## POPIA — Protection of Personal Information Act (South Africa)

POPIA is South Africa's primary data protection legislation, aligned closely with GDPR principles. myPilotPost is designed to comply fully with POPIA's eight conditions for lawful processing.

| POPIA Condition | How myPilotPost Complies |
|---|---|
| Accountability | We take responsibility for all personal information we process |
| Processing limitation | We collect only what is necessary for the platform to function |
| Purpose specification | Data is used only for the purposes stated at collection |
| Further processing limitation | We do not repurpose data beyond stated uses |
| Information quality | We maintain accurate records and enable correction requests |
| Openness | This Trust Center documents all processing activities |
| Security safeguards | Encryption, isolation, access controls, and monitoring in place |
| Data subject participation | Users can access, correct, and delete their data on request |

Our POPIA Information Officer contact: **privacy@mypilotpost.com**

## PAIA — Promotion of Access to Information Act (South Africa)

PAIA gives South African residents the right to request access to records held by private bodies. A PAIA manual is available upon request.

- To request access to records: **privacy@mypilotpost.com** — subject line: *PAIA Request*
- We respond to all PAIA requests within the legislated timeframe (30 days, extendable by 30 days in complex cases)
- We will notify you if a request is refused and provide reasons as required by the Act

## Cookie & Analytics Consent

### Cookie Categories

| Category | Description & Basis |
|---|---|
| Strictly Necessary | Required for the platform to function — no consent required |
| Analytics (GA4) | Usage analytics — consent required before activation |
| Functional | Remembering preferences — consent required |
| Marketing | Not used at v1 |

### How Consent Works

- A consent banner is displayed on first visit
- Google Analytics is not initialised until analytics consent is given
- Consent preferences are stored and respected across sessions
- Users can change or withdraw consent at any time via the cookie settings panel
- IP anonymization is enabled for all analytics tracking

## Email Compliance

- All marketing emails include an unsubscribe link
- Transactional emails (password reset, delivery notifications) are exempt from marketing opt-out
- Sender identity and physical address are included in all commercial emails
- Unsubscribe requests are processed within 5 business days
- Email lists are never sold or shared with third parties

## How to Submit a Data Request

> To exercise any of your data rights, email **privacy@mypilotpost.com**. Include your account email and the nature of your request. We acknowledge all requests within 5 business days and complete them within 30 days.

### Types of Requests We Handle

- **Access request** — receive a full export of your personal data
- **Deletion request** — delete your account and all associated personal data
- **Correction request** — correct inaccurate information on your account
- **Portability request** — export your content, analytics data, and brand configuration
- **Objection** — object to specific processing activities
- **Complaint** — if you believe we have not handled your data lawfully

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may lodge a complaint with the **Information Regulator (South Africa):** [inforeg.org.za](https://www.inforeg.org.za)
