Data Handling

What we collect, how we use it, retention periods, and what we never collect — in compliance with POPIA and GDPR.

Thuthu (Pty) Ltd t/a myPilotPost  ·  Reg. No. 2025/102758/07  ·  Information Officer: Littchel Nhlalo Mathuthu  ·  privacy@mypilotpost.com

This page explains what data myPilotPost collects, how it is used, what it is never used for, and how it is protected. Thuthu (Pty) Ltd is committed to data minimisation — we collect only what is necessary to deliver and operate the platform — as required by section 10 of POPIA (purpose specification) and GDPR Article 5(1)(c) (data minimisation). This page supplements our full Privacy Policy. In any conflict between this page and the Privacy Policy, the Privacy Policy governs.

What Data We Collect

Account Data

  • Name and surname
  • Email address
  • Phone number (where provided)
  • Date of birth (to verify 18+ eligibility)
  • Company name, job title and role (where provided)
  • Profile picture (where provided)
  • Authentication identifiers from OAuth providers
  • Password hash where email/password auth is used — passwords are never stored in plaintext

Brand & Workspace Data

  • Brand name, description, voice config
  • Platform targeting preferences and content goals
  • Brand memory events
  • Team member associations

Content Data

  • Social media post drafts and scheduling records
  • Blog post drafts and publication records
  • Campaign configurations and mission briefs
  • Content generation history (prompts and AI-generated outputs — used only within your own brand workspace)

Scheduling & Delivery Data

  • Scheduled post records
  • Delivery results — success, failure, and retry records
  • Delivery job logs

Analytics Data

  • Engagement metrics retrieved from connected platform APIs
  • Delivery success and failure events
  • Content performance comparisons (within your brand workspace only)

OAuth & Integration Metadata

  • Connected account identifiers — we never receive social platform passwords
  • OAuth access tokens — encrypted at rest, scoped per connected account and brand
  • Integration status and health indicators
  • Permission scopes granted at OAuth connection time

Payment Data

Payments are processed by YOCO. We receive and store: subscription tier and plan details; payment status (successful, failed, pending); transaction reference identifiers from YOCO. We do NOT store payment card numbers, CVV codes, or banking credentials.

Technical & Operational Data

  • API request logs
  • Error logs
  • IP address and device information
  • Browser type and version, operating system
  • Pages visited, time spent, navigation metadata
  • Cookie and tracking technology data — subject to consent choices

What We Do NOT Collect

  • Social media account passwords — OAuth means we never receive them
  • Private messages, DMs, or inbox content from any connected social platform
  • Browsing activity outside of myPilotPost
  • Device fingerprints or persistent cross-site tracking identifiers
  • Third-party advertising profiles or ad network data
  • Data from platforms you have not explicitly connected to your myPilotPost account
  • Content data from other customers or brands
  • Biometric data or government-issued identity document numbers (except where legally required)

How Data Is Used

Purpose Data Used Lawful Basis
Providing and operating the platform Account data, brand data, content data, scheduling records Contract

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We share data only in limited circumstances: YOCO (payment processing); Cloudflare (infrastructure provider); Connected social platforms (when you publish content); Legal requirement (valid court order); Business transfers (users notified in advance).

Data Retention

Data Category Retention Period
Active account and brand data Retained while your account is active
Content drafts and scheduling records Retained while your account is active
Delivery logs 90 days from delivery date
Analytics events Duration of active subscription
Payment transaction references 5 years (tax and accounting obligations under South African law)
Security and API logs 90 days
Email consent records Duration of account plus 3 years
Data following account deletion Personal data removed within 30 days of confirmed deletion request

Your Data Rights

  • Access — request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Rectification — request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Deletion / Erasure — request deletion of your account and associated personal data
  • Portability — request an export of your content, analytics data, and brand configuration in a machine-readable format
  • Restriction — request that we limit processing of your data in specific circumstances
  • Objection — object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Withdraw consent — withdraw marketing or analytics consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing

To exercise any right: Email privacy@mypilotpost.com with your account email address and the nature of your request. We will acknowledge within 5 business days and respond fully within 30 days as required by POPIA and GDPR.

If not satisfied: Information Regulator (South Africa): inforeg.org.za  ·  inforeg@justice.gov.za | EU residents: Your local national data protection supervisory authority.
